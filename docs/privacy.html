Privacy Policy — Pluto Phases Timeline

This policy covers the Pluto Phases Timeline widget only. The original "What Was Happening?" widget and the Pluto Phases Gems app each have their own separate privacy policies.

1. Birth Information You Enter

When you enter your birth date, time, UTC offset, and gender, that information is sent to a server-side function ("the timeline service") solely to compute your 3-year timeline for that one request. No birth place, name, or contact information is collected or sent by this step — the name field is optional and stays in your browser only.

2. If You Purchase the 100-Year Timeline

If you click "Buy the Full 100-Year Timeline," the widget opens a pre-filled JotForm in a new tab containing your birth date, time, and gender. Nothing is sent anywhere until you review that form and click Submit yourself — at that point it is sent to JotForm and made available to Sanju Paison at lifeprediction.net so your purchase can be fulfilled.

3. Rate Limiting

To prevent automated abuse, the number of timeline requests a given internet connection (IP address) can make per day may be limited. This is tracked only as a short-lived counter tied to an IP address on a rolling basis and is not linked to any personal information you entered.

4. Third-Party Information

This widget may be used to generate a timeline based on information about someone other than you. If Section 2 applies to that request, the same handling described there applies to that person's information as well.

5. This Website

This widget is designed to be embedded on lifeprediction.net (hosted via GoDaddy / Nicepage). The widget's own code and data are hosted on GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Standard technical data (e.g. IP address, browser type, access times) may be collected automatically by these hosting providers as part of normal web hosting.

6. What This Policy Does Not Cover

This policy covers this widget and the lifeprediction.net website only. It does not cover any third-party service you may be directed to (JotForm, GitHub, Cloudflare, or a payment processor), each of which has its own privacy practices.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the widget after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
